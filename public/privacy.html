Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026. Daily Dose is a beta. This page says what the app actually does.

What we keep

When you play, the app makes a guest account for you (no email, and no name unless you add one) so the server can check your answers and keep one result per game per day. We store your results and what you played: tile turns, guesses, shots and answers.

If you add a name for your friends, we keep it (up to 20 characters). Everyone also gets a friend code. When you type someone's code, we note the time of that try for one day, so codes can't be guessed (10 tries an hour).

If you add an email and password, we keep your email and a scrambled (hashed) password. We never see or store your password itself.

In Mind Meld, what you type is stored with your own result. Other players only ever see answers from our approved list, counted together, never your typing or your name.

What others see

Everyone: totals only. How many people played, the share of guesses in each range, how many people gave a listed answer. Nothing that points to you.

Friends: only people you've added by typing each other's friend code. They see your name and tonight's results (scores, like "Lit in 1:23"), never your answers or guesses. You can remove a friend any time on the Friends screen, and they stop seeing you.

What we don't do

No ads. No selling or sharing your data. No tracking across other sites. No location.

Where it lives

Results, names, friends and accounts are stored with Supabase. The site is hosted by Vercel. Your phone also keeps a copy of your progress so the app works offline. Badges and stats are worked out on your phone from your results.

Age

Daily Dose is for people 13 and over.

Deleting

Clearing the app's data on your phone removes the local copy. To delete an account and its results, contact us and we'll remove it.

Contact

Message the person who shared the beta with you. A support email will be listed here before launch.
