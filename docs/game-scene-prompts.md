# Game scene prompts (ChatGPT, 2026-11-20)

One backdrop per game, all spots inside the same café as the home screen. Until they're done, each game uses
the blurred home picture tinted in its colour (`tools/game-bg.py`).

**How to use:** in ChatGPT, attach the home picture `design/cafe/art/gpt/Daily_Dose_Assets/01_Background_Phone_Day.png` (the tall
day scene) so it copies the style, then paste the shared part plus one game's line. One image per chat message.
Send the six pictures back and I'll cut, size and wire them in.

## Shared part (paste first every time)

> Pixel art background for a mobile game, portrait 9:16 (1024×1792). Match the attached picture exactly: same
> pixel size, same chunky dark-brown outlines, same warm cosy palette and soft lighting. It's another spot inside
> the same small café. This is a backdrop: a cream card with the game will sit over the middle 60% of the picture,
> so keep the centre calm and simple (plain wall, window, or soft shapes, nothing busy), and put the details at
> the top, the bottom and the edges. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no people in the middle.
> Full-bleed scene, no border, no magenta.

## One line per game

- **The Shot (gold, #FFC93C):** a close-up of the espresso bar: a shiny brass espresso machine at the bottom edge
  with a single small shot glass under the spout, a chalk tally board high on the wall, warm golden spotlight from
  above, a few coffee beans scattered on the counter. Feeling: one moment, focus.
- **Rush (red, #F0452B):** the morning rush seen from behind the counter: a long queue of customer silhouettes
  out the door at the top edge, order tickets clipped on a rail, a wall clock, steam everywhere, warm red and
  orange tones. Feeling: busy, fast, panic in a fun way.
- **Blend (coral, #FF8A5B):** the smoothie and blender corner by a big sunny window: two blenders and fruit bowls
  on the bottom counter, hanging plants at the top, a pastel coral wall. Feeling: bright, playful, guessing what
  everyone else is thinking.
- **Drop (green, #1F9A5D):** the café's back room after closing: a green felt card table at the bottom with a
  small pile of coffee-bean "chips" and a deck of cards, a low hanging lamp at the top, dark wood walls. Feeling:
  nerve, bank it or bust.
- **Missing Link (blue, #3D6FD9):** a cosy reading nook: tall bookshelves on both edges, a cork board at the top
  with pinned notes joined by red string, an armchair and a mug at the bottom, cool blue evening light through a
  window. Feeling: the "aha" of connecting things.
- **Ladder (tan, #C9A46A):** the tall shelf wall where the beans are kept: a wooden rolling ladder on the right
  edge, shelves of jars and coffee sacks going up out of the top, a calm cream wall in the middle, soft morning
  light. Feeling: calm, one step at a time.
